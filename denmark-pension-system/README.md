# Denmark Pension System

## Overview

Group presentation analyzing the structure, funding, and sustainability of the Danish pension system, one of the highest-ranked systems worldwide according to the Mercer CFA Institute Global Pension Index.

The analysis covers the system's three pillars, its historical evolution, and its adequacy, sustainability, and long-term challenges.

## Content

- **Demographics**: population structure, ageing trends, life expectancy and dependency ratios
- **Evolution of the pension system**: key reforms from the introduction of the Folkepension (1956) to the current retirement age framework
- **1st Pillar**: Folkepension (residence-based state pension) and ATP (statutory defined-contribution scheme), including contribution and payout mechanics
- **2nd and 3rd Pillars**: occupational pensions (collectively agreed, defined-contribution) and voluntary private pension savings
- **Full system**: total pension assets by pillar, asset allocation, and a combined retirement income projection
- **Variant careers**: impact of parental leave, unemployment, and self-employment on pension accrual, and early retirement pathways
- **Taxation**: tax treatment of contributions, returns and benefits across pillars
- **Strengths and weaknesses**: system ranking on adequacy, sustainability and integrity, and structural challenges (system maturity, dependence on migration)
